# Logo Brief Template (for Claude Design)

How to use it: fill in the square brackets, delete anything that doesn't apply, then paste everything below the line into Claude Design (claude.ai/design). The more specific you are about the business and what to avoid, the better the concepts.

---

I need first-round logo concepts for a [type of business, e.g. "UK private healthcare clinic"] called **[Business name]**. Please generate 4 to 6 distinct concept directions, each shown as a full logo lockup plus a simplified icon-only mark that could work as a favicon or social avatar.

### The business

[Two or three sentences: who runs it, what they do, where they operate, how long they've been going, any qualifications or credentials that matter.]

The single most important differentiator: [what makes this business different from its competitors]. The logo should feel like [the impression you want, e.g. "a specialist with real expertise, not a high-street generalist"].

### Audience

[Who the customers are: age range, what problem brings them in, how they find the business. If there's more than one audience (e.g. customers AND professional referrers), say so and say what each needs to feel.]

### Brand personality

- [e.g. Specialist, not generalist]
- [e.g. Warm and human, but credible]
- [e.g. Calm, reassuring, precise]
- [e.g. Established, not new or trendy]
- [any regional or cultural feel you want]

Avoid any feel of: [the kinds of business you must NOT be mistaken for].

### Visual direction

**Colour palette (please use these or very close variants):**
- [Colour name]: `[#hex]` (primary)
- [Colour name]: `[#hex]` (secondary)
- [Colour name]: `[#hex]` (accent, small highlights only)
- White and off-white for breathing room

[If there's no palette yet, describe the mood instead and ask it to propose one.]

**Style directions to explore (please generate one concept for each direction, plus two of your own):**

1. **Wordmark-led**: a refined wordmark with a small mark. Something that could sit on a letterhead or formal document without looking out of place.
2. **Monogram**: the initials in a clean geometric form. [Any subtle reference you'd like, without being literal.]
3. **Abstract**: a stylised, minimal reference to [what the business does]. Must be abstract and elegant, never a literal illustration.
4. **Heritage**: something closer to a crest or seal, a modern take on a traditional form. [Delete if it doesn't suit the business.]

**Technical requirements:**
- Must work at very small sizes (favicon, email signature)
- Must work in a single colour (for print and invoices)
- Horizontal lockup AND stacked lockup for each concept
- Clean enough to embroider on a polo shirt or print on business cards
- Typography suggestions to go alongside the mark

### What NOT to do

- [The obvious clichés for this industry, e.g. "no cartoon feet or shoe icons"]
- [Generic symbols to avoid, e.g. "no medical crosses or stethoscopes"]
- No swooshes or motion lines
- No gradients that can't survive single-colour print
- [Any look you don't want, e.g. "no tech startup feel"]
- [Competitor styles to steer clear of]

### Deliverables from this first round

Please produce:
1. 4 to 6 distinct concept directions as described above
2. For each: full logo lockup in colour, icon-only mark and single-colour version
3. A short paragraph per concept explaining the design thinking and what it signals
4. A recommendation on which two concepts to develop further and why

I'll review, pick directions, and come back for round two.

---

Tip: if you're going to use the logo as a Google Ads business logo, check Google's current image size and format requirements before round two and give them to Claude Design as part of that brief.
