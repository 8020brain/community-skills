# Website Content Writer

Writes website copy that converts visitors into customers. Every word earns its place. No padding, no filler, no corporate speak.

## What You Need From the User

Before writing, check for the following files in the project root and read them if they exist:

- **`voice-dna.md`** - how the brand writes. Tone, vocabulary, style, what to avoid.
- **`brand-voice.md`** or any brand guidelines document - same purpose, different name.
- **`avatars.md`**, **`customer-avatar.md`**, or any audience/persona file - who the copy is written for, their problems, motivations, and language.

If none of these exist, ask for the following before writing:

1. **Business name** and what they do
2. **Target customer** - who visits the site, what problem they're solving, and how they describe it in their own words
3. **Page** - which page is this copy for (home, about, services, contact, landing)
4. **Tone preference** - professional, friendly, bold, minimal (default to professional but approachable)
5. **Key differentiators** - what makes them different from competitors (2-3 things)
6. **CTA action** - what should visitors do (call, email, book, buy, enquire)

The more the user can share about their audience, the better the copy. If they have any existing brand documentation, ask them to share it.

## Writing Principles

### Headlines

- Lead with the benefit, not the feature
- Answer "what's in it for me?" from the visitor's perspective
- Keep under 10 words
- Avoid clever wordplay that sacrifices clarity

| Weak | Strong |
|---|---|
| "Welcome to Smith Plumbing" | "Reliable Plumbing When You Need It Most" |
| "Our Services" | "How We Help Your Business Grow" |
| "About Our Company" | "Built on 15 Years of Getting Results" |
| "Contact Us" | "Let's Talk About Your Project" |

### Body Copy

- Short paragraphs (2-3 sentences max)
- Use "you" and "your" more than "we" and "our"
- Focus on outcomes the customer gets, not processes you follow
- One idea per paragraph
- Break up text with subheadings every 2-3 paragraphs
- Bullet points for lists of 3+ items

### CTAs

- Action verbs: Get, Start, Book, Claim, Download, Join
- First person when possible: "Get My Free Quote" beats "Get Your Free Quote"
- Specific to the outcome: "Book a Free Consultation" not "Submit"
- Pair with a supporting line that reduces friction: "No obligation. Takes 2 minutes."

### Social Proof Copy

- Testimonials: keep to 2-3 sentences. Include name, role/company, and specific result if possible
- Stats: use real numbers. "Helped 200+ local businesses" beats "Helped many businesses"
- Client logos: label as "Trusted by" or "Worked with", not "Our clients include"

## Page-Specific Copy Guidelines

### Homepage

| Section | Word Count | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| Hero headline | 5-10 words | Primary benefit or value proposition |
| Hero subheadline | 15-25 words | Expand on the headline, add specificity |
| Services overview | 30-50 words per service | What it is and who it helps |
| Social proof | 20-40 words | One strong testimonial or stat |
| Final CTA section | 20-30 words | Urgency or reinforcement + CTA button |

Total homepage copy: 300-500 words.

### About Page

| Section | Word Count | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| Opening statement | 30-50 words | Who you are and what you believe |
| Story | 80-120 words | How you got here, why you do this |
| What makes you different | 50-80 words | 3-4 bullet points |
| Team bios | 40-60 words each | Role, experience, one human detail |

Total about page copy: 250-400 words.

### Service Page

| Section | Word Count | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| Hero | 20-30 words | What the service is and who it helps |
| Description | 60-100 words | What's included, how it works |
| Benefits | 30-50 words | 3-5 bullet points of outcomes |
| Process | 40-60 words | 3-5 numbered steps |
| FAQ | 30-50 words per question | Address objections and common queries |

Total per service page: 200-350 words.

### Contact Page

Keep it minimal:
- Heading: 3-6 words
- Supporting line: 10-20 words ("Have a question? Fill in the form and we'll get back to you within 24 hours.")
- Form labels: clear and short (Name, Email, Phone, Message)
- Submit button: specific ("Send Your Message", "Request a Callback")

### Landing Page

| Section | Word Count | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| Hero | 30-50 words | Problem + solution + CTA |
| Problem section | 40-60 words | Describe the pain point vividly |
| Solution section | 50-80 words | Position your offer as the answer |
| Benefits | 40-60 words | 3-5 outcomes with specifics |
| Testimonial | 20-40 words | One strong, specific quote |
| Risk reversal | 15-25 words | Guarantee, no obligation, free trial |
| Final CTA | 20-30 words | Reinforce the action |

Total landing page copy: 250-400 words.

## Tone

- Professional but human. Sounds like a competent person talking, not a brochure
- No buzzwords: innovative, synergy, cutting-edge, world-class, bespoke (unless the user's industry genuinely uses it)
- No exclamation marks in body copy. One per page maximum, and only on a CTA if it fits
- Contractions are fine (we're, you'll, it's)
- Active voice. "We build websites" not "Websites are built by us"


## Important

- Every page must answer: what do you do, who do you do it for, and what should I do next
- Homepage copy must make sense to someone who has never heard of the business
- Do not use Lorem ipsum or filler. Every word should be real, usable copy
- If the user hasn't provided enough detail, write the copy with clear [placeholders] and tell them exactly what to fill in
- Keep total copy per page lean. Visitors scan, they don't read essays
