# Website Builder Skill Pack

Built by **Zara Imrie** at Clever Operators (cleveroperators.com) and shared free with the Ads to AI community.

## How to use it

Ten Claude skills that take you from a blank folder to a launch-ready WordPress site using Claude Code and Local WP. Open the `guide` folder to get started, then say "Build me a website".

## A few things to know

- **Review everything Claude generates before you publish it.** AI can produce code and copy that has errors or doesn't fit your business. Test in Local WP and read the copy before you go live.
- **You run this on your own Anthropic account**, so you cover your own API usage. Set a spending limit before a long session.
- **Provided as-is, with no warranty.** There's no guarantee the output will be error-free or that your site will rank or convert. Once your site is live, its content, accuracy, and legal compliance are your responsibility.
- **Back up your Local WP site** as you go.
