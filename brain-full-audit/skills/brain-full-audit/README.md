# Brain Full Audit Skill

Audits your AI brain workspace itself (not your business) across eight dimensions: security, cost and token use, efficiency, structure, reliability, data and privacy, rule compliance, and design quality. It produces a scored report with prioritised fixes and changes nothing until you approve specific findings.

## Install

1. Copy the `brain-full-audit` folder into your brain at `.claude/skills/brain-full-audit/`
2. That's it. No configuration needed; the skill discovers what is in your brain rather than assuming.

## Run

Open a Claude Code session in your brain folder and say:

> audit this brain

(or "full brain audit" / "audit the build")

Note: this is different from "run brain audit", which audits your business processes for automation opportunities. This one checks the workspace build itself.

## What you get

- A scorecard rating each of the eight dimensions Red, Amber, or Green
- A findings table where every finding cites the exact file as evidence
- Quick wins (under 15 minutes each) separated from bigger strategic improvements
- A top 5 priority list

The report saves into your brain. Nothing is fixed, moved, or deleted until you approve findings by their ID, and even approved removals go to your trash/ folder, never deleted directly.

## Good cadence

Run it after any big build session, or monthly. The audit catches drift early: broken paths after renames, duplicate skills, secrets that crept into git, and instructions that contradict each other.
