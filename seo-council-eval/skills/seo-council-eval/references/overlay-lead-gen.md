# Vertical overlay: lead-gen and topical authority

For service pages, pillar and hub pages, spoke articles, and brand-building content. The WHO home turf: this overlay assumes the entity-brand-blueprint and topical-map skills produced (or will produce) the architecture this page lives in. Flex seat: Ross Hudgens or Ryan Law; add Talia Wolf's conversion seat for service and landing pages.

## Weight shifts

| Dimension | Core | Lead-gen |
|---|---|---|
| Entity and blueprint conformance | 25% | 30% |
| GEO / AEO retrieval | 20% | 20% |
| Search intent match | 20% | 15% |
| E-E-A-T | 15% | 15% |
| On-page mechanics | 10% | 10% |
| Strategic fit | 10% | 10% |

Rationale: topical authority is cumulative — each page's conformance to the map is what makes the whole cluster rank, so conformance weighs heaviest here.

## Dimension additions

**Entity coverage** also checks: the page holds its exact hub-or-spoke assignment (a spoke that grows into a second pillar is a defect, not a bonus); attribute coverage advances the site's EAV completeness rather than repeating what a sibling already covers.

**Strategic fit** also checks: the trust-building arc — where this page sits in the stranger-to-lead journey, and whether the ask matches the stage (a top-funnel spoke pushing a sales call scores down; one capturing nothing at all also scores down); authority flows toward the money pages through links, not just existence.

**E-E-A-T** also checks: the page demonstrates the expertise the service sells (a marketing agency's article should itself be evidence of the craft); author identity consistent with the entity blueprint's credential claims.

**GEO / AEO** also checks: the page is quotable as the *brand's* answer — passages an assistant would attribute to the brand by name, which is what builds entity-level authority in AI answers.

## Lead-gen severity conditions

Each condition names the **one dimension that scores it**. If it matters to your
dimension too, say so in your findings, but do not take the numeric hit unless
the tag is yours. One defect should move one number.

- **[onpage] Major (subtract 1 to 2):** A spoke article with no link to its hub, or a hub that doesn't link its spokes.
- **[geo] Moderate (prevents an 8):** Content indistinguishable from the top-ranking generic answer, adding no brand-attributable point of view.
- **[fit] Moderate (prevents an 8):** Capture path absent on a page whose map brief says it converts.
