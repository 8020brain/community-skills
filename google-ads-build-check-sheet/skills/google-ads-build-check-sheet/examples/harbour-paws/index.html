Why the numbers beside each line matter

Google gives you 30 characters for a headline and 90 for a description, and charges the same whether you use them or not. The number is what each line uses. Across your ads the descriptions average 86 of the 90 available.

06

Your ads

3 groups of ads, each answering one thing people type into Google. Google builds each ad by picking from a pool of headlines and descriptions, then testing the combinations, so the wording is raw material rather than a finished ad. Each ad gets up to 8 headlines and 4 descriptions. Tap any ad group below to open it.

Waiting on your approval

None of this is in your Google Ads account yet. Once you are happy with the wording we build it, paused, and nothing runs until we switch it on together. Changes are easiest now, because Google will not let us edit an ad in place once it is running.
