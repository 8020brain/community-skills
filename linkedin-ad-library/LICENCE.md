# LinkedIn Ad Library

Built by **Thuha Wright** and shared free with the Ads2AI community.

## How to use it

Connect the Notion connector, then say "set up my LinkedIn ad library". Claude asks which
Notion page to put it under, builds the database and its views, and remembers it. After that,
drop in a LinkedIn ad screenshot (plus the Ad Library link if you have one) and say "save this
LinkedIn ad".

## Want more than LinkedIn?

Thuha's skill was the starting point for **Ad Swipe File**, which now ships in the brain. It
covers Meta, Google, YouTube, LinkedIn and TikTok, saves into your brain by default (Notion
stays an option), grabs ads straight from public ad library links, and gives you a gallery to
browse, rate and filter by hook. Update your brain to get it, then say "save this ad".

## A few things to know

- **It writes to your own Notion**, using your own Notion connection and your own Claude usage.
